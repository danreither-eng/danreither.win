Best Wines.

Six recommended reds from Italy, South Africa and California, from classic old-world structure to whiskey-barrel-aged crowd pleasers.

How to read this list

Notes describe the style and what to expect; vintages and bottlings vary, so check the label.

Old-world Italian

Rompicollo and Ripasso: cherry, herbs, savory depth, and food-friendly acidity.

New-world fruit

Copper Lane and Red Angel Red: ripe dark fruit and a softer, rounder style.

Barrel-aged & bold

1000 Stories and Apothic Inferno: bourbon and whiskey barrel influence with vanilla, toast and spice.

The lineup

Six reds worth pouring

01

Poggio al Tufo Rompicollo 2022

Toscana, Italy · Sangiovese & Cabernet Sauvignon

A Tuscan red from Tommasi Family Estates that pairs Sangiovese’s bright cherry and savory herbs with Cabernet’s structure. Expect dark cherry, plum, a touch of spice and firm but friendly tannins.

Best for: pasta, grilled meats, and Italian dinners

About Tommasi →
02

Valpolicella Ripasso

Veneto, Italy · Corvina-based blend · producer not shown on the label photo

Ripasso is Valpolicella re-fermented on the pomace left from Amarone, which adds body, depth and a hint of dried-fruit richness. Typical notes are cherry, plum, cocoa and gentle spice with a smooth, savory finish. Often called “baby Amarone.”

Best for: a step up from everyday red without Amarone prices

About Valpolicella →
03

Copper Lane Reserve Shiraz-Merlot

Coastal Region, Western Cape, South Africa · Shiraz & Merlot

A soft, fruit-forward South African blend. Shiraz brings dark berry and peppery spice while Merlot rounds it out with plum and a plush, easy texture.

Best for: burgers, barbecue, and relaxed weeknight pours

About South African wine →
04

F. Stephen Millier Black Label “Red Angel Red” 2023

Calaveras County, California · red blend

A small-production California red from the Sierra foothills. Expect ripe dark fruit, a little spice and a smooth, medium-bodied finish typical of Calaveras County reds.

Best for: a distinctive, small-winery bottle to share

05

California · Cabernet Sauvignon

A bold Cabernet aged in used bourbon barrels, which adds vanilla, caramel, toasted oak and a hint of smoke over blackberry and dark cherry. A natural bridge between wine and whiskey lovers.

Best for: bourbon fans exploring wine; steak and BBQ

Serving tips

Getting more from every bottle

Serve slightly cool

Reds show best around 60 to 65°F. Chill briefly if the room is warm.

Let it breathe

Ripasso and bold Cabernets open up after 20 to 30 minutes in the glass or a decanter.

Pair with food

Italian reds love tomato and grilled meats; barrel-aged wines suit barbecue and steak.

Drink responsibly

Enjoy in moderation, never drink and drive, and only if you are of legal drinking age.
