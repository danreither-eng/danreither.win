Lehigh Valley & Nearby Hiking

Find a trail close to home, from Lehigh Valley overlooks to the Delaware Water Gap.

Choose a place

  • Lehigh Gorge State Park: follow the river on the Lehigh Gorge Trail; choose a trailhead and turnaround that match your time and fitness.
  • Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area: explore river corridors and ridge trails. Some routes have steep, rocky climbs; consult the park's current trail guidance.
  • Hawk Mountain Sanctuary: choose a lookout or a longer rocky loop. Check visitor hours, admission, trail conditions and seasonal raptor activity.

Plan before you go

Confirm the trail's current status, route length, elevation, parking and permitted uses with the land manager. Allow extra daylight, bring water and a downloaded map, and tell someone your plan. Turn around for storms, ice or conditions beyond your experience.

Stay on marked trails, carry out waste and check for ticks. Leave pets at home where rules prohibit them, and keep them leashed where required.

Trusted trail information

  • Pennsylvania DCNR: Lehigh Gorge State Park
  • National Park Service: Delaware Water Gap
  • Hawk Mountain Sanctuary
  • National Weather Service forecast
