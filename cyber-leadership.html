Cybersecurity Leadership & Mentoring

Develop people, explain risk clearly, and build security programs that support the mission.

Build the team

Clarify responsibilities and decision rights, make space for learning, and share feedback regularly. Use role expectations and development plans rather than treating certifications as the only measure of progress.

Mentoring can begin with a regular conversation: agree on a goal, listen to the mentee's context, suggest a small next step, and revisit what worked. Respect boundaries and avoid sharing confidential information.

Communicate risk in business terms

  • Describe the service or outcome at risk, the plausible impact, and the uncertainty.
  • Present practical options with cost, tradeoffs, an accountable owner and a decision needed.
  • Track a small set of measures tied to outcomes, and explain what they do and do not show.
  • Exercise incident decisions with business partners before a crisis.
