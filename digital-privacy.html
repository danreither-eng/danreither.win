Security essentials

Digital Privacy for Everyday Life

A practical starting point for safer accounts, devices, and personal information.

Protect your most important accounts

Start with email, financial accounts and your mobile carrier. Use a unique password for each account and a reputable password manager. Enable multifactor authentication; passkeys or security keys can provide phishing-resistant sign-in where supported.

Save recovery codes in a secure place separate from the device used to sign in. Review recovery email addresses and phone numbers so they remain current.

Reduce exposure

  • Install operating-system, browser and app updates promptly; use a screen lock and device encryption when available.
  • Review app permissions and location sharing. Remove apps and account connections you no longer use.
  • Verify unexpected messages through a known, independently obtained contact method before clicking or sending money. Never share sign-in codes with a caller.
  • Back up important data and occasionally test a restore.

Reliable starting points

  • CISA: Secure Our World
  • FTC: identity theft and online security
  • FIDO Alliance: passkeys
