Jon R. Taylor, CISSP

Security Architect · Cloud Security · Detection Engineering

Connect with Jon on LinkedIn →

Jon is a hands-on security architect and senior security engineer with 15 years of IT and security experience across hybrid Azure, AWS, Microsoft 365, and on-premises environments. His work spans detection engineering, cloud and identity hardening, incident response, and security automation. He brings a practical, people-first approach: security controls should work for the teams who rely on them.

What he brings

  • Built a security monitoring and response capability from the ground up, unifying cloud, endpoint, and on-premises telemetry.
  • Develops and tunes SIEM detections mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, balancing useful coverage with lower false-positive noise.
  • Leads incident response from triage through containment, recovery, and lessons learned.
  • Secures Azure, AWS, Microsoft 365, identity, network, and infrastructure-as-code environments.

Selected outcomes

  • Reported 70% higher early detection of security events after introducing detection and response capabilities.
  • Reported reductions of 65% in mean time to detect and 70% in mean time to respond.
  • Implemented organization-wide MFA and Conditional Access, reaching full workforce adoption in one month.
  • Created incident-response playbooks and exercises that improved readiness and recovery practices.

Areas of expertise

Microsoft Sentinel · Splunk · Wazuh/Elastic · Microsoft Defender · Azure and AWS security · Entra ID · incident response · threat hunting · Terraform · Python · PowerShell · MITRE ATT&CK and ATLAS

Community service

Jon serves as Membership Chair on the ISC2 Philadelphia Chapter board, after previously supporting the chapter as webmaster. He also mentors Temple University students in IT auditing and cybersecurity.

Credentials: CISSP · CCSP · Microsoft Azure Security Engineer (AZ-500) · AWS Certified Security – Specialty · CCNA
