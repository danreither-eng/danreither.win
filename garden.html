Local timing

Frost dates and how to use this guide

Use roughly May 5 as the last spring frost and October 15 as the first fall frost near Easton, Pennsylvania. These are planning estimates, not guarantees; soil temperature, the forecast and your microclimate matter.

Permanent plantings

Jersey asparagus, strawberries and blueberries. Choose the site carefully, because these stay for years.

Annual vegetables

San Marzano tomatoes, eggplant, pickling cucumbers, Brussels sprouts, broccoli, cauliflower, onions and garlic. Rotate beds every year.

Herbs

Basil, parsley, dill, cilantro, thyme, oregano, rosemary, chives and sage. Many double as good garden neighbors.

Season at a glance

A planning rhythm for the Lehigh Valley

Jan–FebPlan beds, order seed, start onions and brassicas indoors.
MarStart tomatoes and eggplant indoors; prepare beds; plant onion sets as soil allows.
AprPlant asparagus crowns and strawberries; sow cool crops; transplant hardy starts.
May–JunAfter frost: tomatoes, eggplant, basil, cucumbers. Mulch and trellis.
Jul–AugHarvest summer crops; start fall brassicas; keep watering steady.
Sep–OctHarvest fall crops; plant garlic in mid to late October; mulch beds.
