Heritage Goods Care & Repair

Make well-used footwear, clothing, and watches last longer with sensible care and professional service.

Leather footwear

Brush off dirt, let wet shoes dry naturally away from direct heat, and use shoe trees where appropriate. Follow the maker's instructions for the specific leather before conditioning or polishing; test products in an inconspicuous spot first. Suede and specialty leathers need different care.

A reputable cobbler can assess worn heels, soles and stitching. Ask about construction, repair options and cost before work begins.

Denim and outerwear

Follow the garment's care label. Repair small tears early and consider a tailor or visible mending for worn areas. Waxed jackets have particular cleaning and re-waxing requirements; avoid assuming the instructions for ordinary cotton apply.

Mechanical watches

Keep the crown secured as directed, avoid unnecessary shocks and have water resistance checked by a qualified professional when needed. Service intervals and water-resistance limits vary by model; consult the manufacturer or a trusted watchmaker.
