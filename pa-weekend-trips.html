Pennsylvania Weekend Itineraries

Pair outdoor time, local food, and an overnight stay into an easy weekend from the Lehigh Valley.

Lehigh Gorge & Jim Thorpe

Day one: walk a manageable section of the Lehigh Gorge Trail, then explore Jim Thorpe and stay nearby. Day two: visit a local attraction or choose another short trail before heading home. Check parking, weather and trail conditions first.

Delaware Water Gap & Upper Delaware

Day one: choose a hike or river-view stop in Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area and stay in the surrounding region. Day two: explore the Upper Delaware corridor or take a scenic drive. River access and mountain routes can be seasonal or weather dependent.

Bucks County & the Delaware River

Day one: explore river towns and a canal-side walk, then stay at a local inn. Day two: add a winery visit or another outdoor stop. Arrange a designated driver or hired transportation for any tasting; never drink and drive.

Plan your trip

These are flexible ideas rather than reservations or timed routes. Verify hours, availability, admission and road conditions before leaving; select stops that fit your group and the weather.
