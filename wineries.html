Wine country getaway

The Finger Lakes, New York

The deep lakes help moderate a cool growing climate. Riesling ranges from bone-dry to sweet; look also for Gewürztraminer, Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir and sparkling wines. Seneca, Keuka and Cayuga each make a rewarding base for a tasting trip.

Seneca Lake

Vineyards line both shores between Geneva and Watkins Glen. Choose one side for the day rather than trying to circle the whole lake.

Seneca Lake Wine Trail →

Keuka Lake

The Y-shaped lake has scenic vineyard slopes near Hammondsport and Penn Yan, with historic producers and cool-climate whites.

Keuka Lake Wine Trail →

Cayuga Lake

Explore lakeside wineries north of Ithaca, including several along the western shore near Ovid and Romulus.

A logical Finger Lakes winery tour

Start in Hammondsport and work east over three relaxed days. This suggested order visits all six wineries above while crossing Seneca Lake just once, at Watkins Glen. Reverse it if approaching from the east; these are planning routes, not guaranteed fastest drives.

Day 1 · Keuka Lake

Start at Heron Hill’s Hammondsport winery, then visit Dr. Konstantin Frank on the west side of Keuka Lake. Stay near Hammondsport or Watkins Glen.

Map the Keuka stops →

Day 2 · Seneca Lake

Begin at Hermann J. Wiemer on the west shore. Travel south through Watkins Glen, then north to Wagner Vineyards on the east shore; consider staying near Lodi or Ovid.

Map the Seneca stops →

Day 3 · Cayuga Lake

From the Lodi area, cross the ridge toward Ovid and visit Hosmer Winery and Sheldrake Point on Cayuga’s west shore. Finish near Ithaca or return toward your lodging.

Map the Cayuga stops →
