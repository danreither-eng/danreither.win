Best Bourbons.

Eight recommended Kentucky-style bourbons, from sweet and approachable to barrel-proof and oak-driven, plus a few rare bottles worth knowing.

Bourbon in a nutshell

By US law, bourbon is made from at least 51% corn and aged in new charred oak containers. That is why it leans toward caramel, vanilla, and toasted oak.

Sweet & soft

Wheated recipes like Maker’s Mark replace rye with wheat: honey, vanilla, pastry, and less spice.

Spicy & structured

Higher-rye recipes such as Four Roses and Wild Turkey add cinnamon, pepper, and a drier finish.

Reading the label

Bottled-in-Bond: one distillery, one season, aged at least 4 years, 100 proof. Barrel proof / cask strength: bottled undiluted. Single barrel: each bottle from one barrel, so batches vary.

The Top Eight

Recommended bourbons

01

Woodford Reserve Double Oaked

Kentucky · 90.4 proof

Rich, sweet, and exceptionally approachable. A second maturation in a deeply toasted, lightly charred oak barrel creates layers of caramel, vanilla, maple syrup, toasted marshmallow, and dark chocolate. Prominent oak balanced by creamy sweetness.

Best for: satisfying neat sipping

About Woodford Reserve →
02

Four Roses Single Barrel

Kentucky · 100 proof

Beautifully structured with a more complex, spicy personality. Ripe fruit, floral notes, and vanilla lead into caramel, cinnamon, toasted oak, and subtle rye spice. Flavorful without being overly sweet, with a long, warming finish.

Best for: balance and character

About Four Roses →
03

Wild Turkey Rare Breed

Kentucky · barrel proof (about 116 proof)

A bold barrel-proof bourbon that delivers substantial flavor. Brown sugar, caramel, vanilla, toasted oak, cinnamon, and dark fruit, with a long finish of pepper, spice, and lingering sweetness. A serious sipper.

Best for: full-bodied, high-proof flavor

About Wild Turkey →
04

Russell’s Reserve 10 Year Old

Kentucky · 90 proof

A mature, traditional bourbon emphasizing oak and balance. Vanilla, caramel, toasted nuts, and gentle leather develop into cinnamon, baking spice, and charred oak. More developed than many entry-level bourbons, with sweetness in check.

Best for: a dependable everyday sipper

About Russell’s Reserve →
05

Elijah Craig Barrel Proof

Kentucky · barrel proof, varies by batch

Powerful and uncompromising, bottled straight from the barrel without dilution. Caramel, dark chocolate, toasted oak, vanilla, and baking spices dominate, with each release showing its own personality. A few drops of water reveal fruit and sweetness.

Best for: experienced bourbon drinkers

About Elijah Craig →
06

Knob Creek 12 Year Old

Kentucky · 100 proof

A robust, oak-forward bourbon with real maturity. Deep caramel, vanilla, roasted nuts, brown sugar, and seasoned oak open into baking spice, a tobacco-like dryness, and a touch of sweetness. The longer finish blends oak, spice, and caramel.

Best for: oak and depth

About Knob Creek →
07

Maker’s Mark Cask Strength

Kentucky · cask strength, varies by batch

A softer, sweeter high-strength bourbon thanks to its wheat-based recipe. Vanilla, caramel, honey, and baked pastry balanced by cinnamon and gentle oak. Full and warming, surprisingly approachable for its proof.

Best for: less rye spice, more sweetness

About Maker’s Mark →
08

Henry McKenna 10 Year Bottled-in-Bond

Kentucky · 100 proof

A traditional single-barrel bourbon with distinctive maturity. Caramel, vanilla, toasted oak, and light fruit lead into cinnamon, pepper, and a little leather. A dry, warm, oak-driven finish and a classic expression of Kentucky bourbon.

Best for: classic bottled-in-bond style

Tasting tips

Getting more from every pour

Neat, water, or rocks

Taste neat first. Barrel-proof bottles often open up with a few drops of water; a large cube slows dilution.

Compare side by side

Pour two bourbons together, such as a wheated and a high-rye, to learn what you like.

Mind the proof

Barrel-proof bourbons can exceed 120 proof. Pour small and sip slowly.

Drink responsibly

Enjoy in moderation, never drink and drive, and only if you are of legal drinking age.
